Clockwork Calc
Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

In short: Clockwork Calc does not collect, store, or share any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and it never connects to the internet.

This policy explains how the Clockwork Calc app for Android and iOS (the "App"), made by Eugene Erokhin, trading as Eugene Makes Apps ("we"), handles information.

Information we collect

None. The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or usage information. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking tools, and it does not ask for any device permissions.

Data on your device

The numbers you enter and the calculation history are kept only in your device's memory while the App is open. They are not saved to storage and are deleted when the App is closed. The App saves only your display settings (time or regular calculator, hours:minutes or decimal, and whether days and seconds are shown) on your device, so they stay the same next time. Nothing ever leaves your device.

Internet access

The App works fully offline and does not request internet access.

Purchases

The App is free to download from Google Play (and, where available, the Apple App Store). If it ever offers optional purchases, the store will handle payment under its own privacy policy. We will never receive your payment details, only the limited sale information the store gives all developers for accounting and tax purposes, such as an order number and the country of purchase.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The App is a general-purpose calculator and is not directed at children. Because it collects no information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the App ever started to collect any data, this policy would be updated first.
